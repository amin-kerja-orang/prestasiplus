## 5. Limits of this matrix

- Nothing was executed. A named test can be failing, skipped (`it.fails`, `it.todo`) or stale; `it.todo` is called out where seen (pipeline-chain, Databank).
- Specs were read by title and, for the rows that mattered, by body; the other titles were not read line by line, so a few "proven" rows rest on the title alone. The risk is highest for rows citing `servability-parity.spec.ts` (fixture size and shapes not read) and `kuiz.spec.ts` (titles still say "published").
- Front-end-only rows are not proof of absence of backend work; they are left to team C / B4 as briefed.
- Files outside apps/api/test and packages (Playwright E2E under e2e/) were not read.
- Branches were read at their current heads; a later push can change a row.
