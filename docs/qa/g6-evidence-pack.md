### 3.5 Why the A1 real-data run fails go/no-go criterion 2

The 25 Oct run is the first look at real `.cloud` values, and it found one root cause with a very large blast radius plus a set of genuine orphans. **Percentages only; the LEAD holds the counts.** Criterion 2 allows at most **0.1 %** rejects for `users` and `schools` and **0.5 %** per other entity.

**One unmapped enum accounts for most of it.** `education_levels` is **100 % rejected** on `UNKNOWN_ENUM stage`: every source value is `Rendah`, `Menengah Rendah` or `Menengah Atas`, and `STAGE_MAP` knows only the v2 names. That single map then cascades: `chapters` **100 %** orphan `education_level_id`, `subject_levels` **100 %**, `practice_answers` **100 %** (about 74 % of it orphan `education_level_id`), `classrooms` **36 %** invalid `education_level_id`. This is a one-line fix with a four-entity payoff, which is why A1-FIX is worth waiting for rather than waiving.

**Other unmapped enums:** `coin_ledger` 11.8 % (33 unmapped `source` codes), `challenges` 33 % (5 unmapped `challenge_type` codes, carrying `challenge_completions` to 25 % orphan), `school_subscriptions` 50 % (`package` = `Pemantauan`), `banners` 60 % (`type` = `FEATURE`, `PROMOTIONAL`), `exam_attempts` 63 % (`status` = `in_progress`).

**Genuine orphans, not a mapping bug:** `classrooms` 61 % orphan `teacher_id` (plus 3 % `school_id`), all of those teachers absent from the source `users` as deleted accounts, which carries `enrolments` to **100 %** orphan `classroom_id`; `practice_sessions` 29 % and `practice_answers` about 26 % orphan `user_id`, also absent from source. These need a **documented exclusion**, not a map.

**By design:** `wallets` 10.7 % `UNSUPPORTED user_id` (non-student roles), `tiers` 100 % `UNSUPPORTED` (reference data, seeded by #230), `users` 0.1 % `agent` (decided: not migrated).

**What passes:** `users` loads **99.9 %**, `ORPHAN_PARENT` on `users` and `schools` is **0** (criterion 3), and `migrate:verify` exits 0 over 39 entities and 16 checks (criterion 1).

**Corrected in rev. 8 - this is not an R39 regression, and the real problem is worse.** Rev. 4 read the accounts carrying a `$2y$` hash **and** the `must_reset_password` flag as contradicting R39. They do not: `transform/secrets.ts:49-50` sets the flag for a shared-default hash or a roster account **while keeping the valid bcrypt**, which is intended, and R39/R39b cover only admin-created, imported and bootstrapped accounts.

**What matters instead: a flagged account cannot get through the front door.** The backend has `PASSWORD_CHANGE_REQUIRED` (`common/errors/api-error.ts`, `modules/auth/password-change.guard.ts`, with conformance and unit tests), but in the frontend the string appears **only in `src/mock/server/respond.ts`** - the mock server. **The SPA has no handling for it**, so every flagged migrated account lands on 403 pages at first login. About 0.5 % of migrated accounts have no usable password at all, and the roster and shared-default accounts are flagged on top of that. The fix is ready as drafts **#226** (the page) and **#357** (the router patch), both waiting on R39-C4 and on a dated freeze exception; the local synthetic evidence is e2e 9 of 9 with them and 3 of 3 failing without.

**And the P11 smoke would not catch it**, because the smoke accounts are v2-born and therefore unflagged by construction. Criterion 9 can pass on 30 Oct while migrated users are locked out. That gap belongs in front of the LEAD at G6, not in the password-reset spike.

**One decision is open that changes what "fails" means here.** The reject percentages can be read against **source rows** or against **eligible rows**, and the LEAD has been asked to pick ("C2: eligible" or "C2: source"); the answer is not in yet. On real A1 data it matters most where the orphan rate is high - 61 % of classes are teacherless - so `classrooms` and `enrolments` move depending on the denominator. The recommendation on the issue is **eligible**, which is the stricter reading. Until it is settled, criterion 2 cannot be scored precisely, and the pack reads it the strict way.

**A1-FIX** is scoped to the `STAGE_MAP` values, the `coin_ledger.source` map (33 codes), the banner `type` map, the `challenge_type` map (which rides #318), `Pemantauan` to `basic`, and documented exclusions for `exam_attempts in_progress`, teacherless classes with their enrolments, the practice orphans and the wallets. Due SEDIA 20:00 MYT on 25 Oct. The re-run restores `pre_etl` and repeats A1.8 to A1.11 on the new sha with the same dump, which is retained only until the A4 sitting on 27 Oct.
