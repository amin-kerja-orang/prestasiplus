## 5. How to read this at 18:00 on 28 Oct

1. Any row in section 2 still reading **missing** is, by the plan's own rule, **not met**. There is no partial credit and no verbal waiver: a waiver has to be written in the issue, with a reason, and only in daylight.
2. **P12 is the hard one**: if any Perlu pengesahan right is unconfirmed, the plan already says the night is a no-go, whatever the rest of the table looks like.
3. **P4 criterion 8 and `PITR off`** are a night STOP with no waiver at all.
4. A no-go leaves `.cloud` untouched and nothing to undo (plan A5, section 6.5). The cost of a no-go is a replan; the cost of a go on an unevidenced row is a migration of real pupil data with no proven way back.
