# SPM trial-paper patterns (2025): question order, difficulty, marks, topic and KBAT spread

- **Slug:** `v2-bijak-spm` (companion: [../requirements/v2-bijak-spm.md](../requirements/v2-bijak-spm.md), US-34 to US-40)
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 | **Method:** manual reading of page images of public trial papers by three browser agents (one lane each), then consolidation. No code, no database, no live site was used.
- **Decision:** TRACKER D75 (save the findings; update the requirements; launch subjects = those with material).
- **Tags:** **[OBS]** = printed on the page and read. **[INF]** = analyst judgement (S level, TP, cognitive level, DSKP mapping, topic). Every S, TP and unit label in this document is [INF] unless marked [OBS].
- **Copyright and privacy:** the papers are state-body trial papers (not LPM), so this document records format and pattern facts only: structure, marks, counts, order, shares. It holds no question text, no mark-scheme text, no copied figures. No person names were recorded; if any had appeared they would be written "[nama]". Per-question working tables are kept outside the repo.

## 1. Purpose

The user worried that papers generated by "Jana Kertas" (Bijak SPM start 2) will not meet the exam standard. This study answers, from real papers:

1. How are questions ordered by number, and by how much information is given versus worked out by the student?
2. How are marks, topics (JSU), cognitive level (TP) and KBAT spread over a paper?
3. Do different issuing bodies agree, so that the pattern can be written as a rule?
4. How does each question map to the DSKP extracts in `docs/reference/dskp/`?

Output: facts for the blueprint (structure, difficulty profile, topic quota, KBAT quota) in the requirements, not a finished JSU.

## 3. Method

### 3.1 Information-load scale (S1-S4) [INF]

| Level | Meaning | Typical command |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Given and direct: the answer is stated or one step from the stimulus | Nyatakan, Namakan, Apakah |
| S2 | Guided: sub-parts lead the student step by step | Hitung, Terangkan, "Tunjukkan bahawa ... Seterusnya ..." |
| S3 | Extract and combine from a stimulus; the method is not given | Jelaskan, Huraikan, multi-step with data |
| S4 | Open or unfamiliar: justify, design, decide, evaluate (KBAT) | Cadangkan, Wajarkan, "tentukan kesesuaian" |

### 3.2 TP mapping [INF]

TP is read from the DSKP descriptors (generic KSSM ladder: TP1 recall, TP2 understand, TP3 apply a simple task, TP4 routine problem, TP5 complex or new situation, TP6 non-routine or creative; Sejarah and Fizik use their own TP wording). The DSKP gives TP per unit or per SK, not per question, so the question-level TP is the analyst's reading of the descriptor wording.

Observed relation between S and TP: S1 is about TP2-3, S2 TP3-4, S3 TP4-5, S4 TP5-6. This is loose: in Matematik K1 (SBP) several S3 items sit at TP5 and no item is S4. A TP threshold therefore does **not** reproduce an S4 share (see section 5.4).

### 3.3 DSKP mapping [INF]

Each question was matched to a unit (and SK/SP where possible) in the DSKP extracts. Units are keyed by (level, code): `T4 3.0` and `T5 3.0` are different units. "OUT" means Tingkatan 1-3 content that no loaded unit covers (not an error of the paper). "Ambiguous" means two units fit.

## 5. Pattern findings

### 5.1 Difficulty by question number: three profiles

| Profile | Papers | Evidence |

| **By Bahagian** | Matematik K2; Matematik Tambahan K1 and K2; Geografi K2; Sains Komputer K1; Sejarah K2 (by sub-part, section 5.2) | Matematik K2: A is S1-S2 (S1+S2 = 78% of marks SBP, 90% Selangor), B is S2-S3 with a few S4 (S3 47% SBP, 40% Selangor), C is the full range ending in S4. | **Theme blocks with mixed difficulty** | Geografi K1 | S by band Q1-10 / 11-20 / 21-30 / 31-40: S1 5/4/1/1, S2 4/5/7/9, S3 1/1/2/0, S4 0; items follow topic blocks (earth, weather and climate, ecosystem, population, settlement, energy, economy), difficulty mixed inside each block; not sorted easy to hard |

### 5.2 Difficulty inside a question: sub-part ramp

- **Sejarah K2 [OBS marks, INF S]:** 9 of 9 questions end on their highest-S part; 8 of 9 are a clean ramp (Q1 is S2, S2, S2, S3). Marks rise with the part: A Q2-4 are 2/4/4 (Q1 is 2/2/2/4); B Q7 and Q9 are 6/6/8; B Q6 and Q8 are 4/8/8 (Q6 corrected by the skema from the paper reading of 6/6/8); B Q5 is 6/?/8 with (b) unresolved between 6 and 8 (section 10.2). All four A questions and 4 of 5 B questions end on a stimulus-based part.
- **Geografi [OBS]:** Nyatakan/Namakan (1-2 marks) then Jelaskan/Terangkan (3-4) then Cadangkan/Wajarkan at the end; Selangor Q3 and Kedah Q5 share the exact 1/3/4/2 split. Bahagian C 6/8/6 or 6/6/8.
- **Fizik K2 [INF]:** recall, read or compare data, relationship, application, calculation, suggest. Q5-Q8 share one template (define, compare two situations, state the relationship, apply, calculate). Q9-Q11 each end with one 10-mark decide/design part (Q8 ends with a 6-mark propose part).
- **Matematik, Matematik Tambahan [INF]:** common, not strict. Guided chains "Tunjukkan bahawa ... Seterusnya ..." (show-that then hence): 8 of 15 questions in Matematik Tambahan K2 SBP, 3 of 15 in K1 SBP. Some ramps are clean (Matematik K2 SBP Q15 S1, S2, S3, S3), some are not (SBP Q13 S1, S2, S3, S2; SBP Q17 ends at S1). In Matematik K2 Bahagian C each lettered part is a different topic with its own mini-ramp; the (d) part carries the most S4.
- **Matematik K2 Bahagian A:** 3-5 mark questions with 1-3 parts leave little room for a ramp.

**Update 2026-10-29 (D76):** the requirements define KBAT by cognitive level (Mengaplikasi, Menganalisis, Menilai, Mencipta, per the DSKP), not by S4 or TP. Shares on that basis are higher than the S4 shares below, so the table is context only and seeds are re-measured from the per-question cognitive levels.

**Caution for seeding:** these shares count S4 marks at **part** level. A quota written as "marks at TP >= 5" gives different numbers: Matematik K1 (SBP) has 6 of 39 items at TP5 (about 15%) although it has no S4 item; Fizik K2 would give 60 of 120 if TP were stored per question (Q9, Q10, Q11 all TP5 or above) but 36 of 120 if stored per leaf part. Seeds must be re-measured on the chosen KBAT basis from the per-question tables, not copied from this table.

Matematik Tambahan K2 SBP Bahagian A runs T4 8.0, T5 2.0, T5 1.0, T4 4.0, T4 1.0, T5 6.0, T4 2.0. Fizik K2 A runs T5 7.0, T4 5.0, T5 6.0, T5 5.0, T5 1.0, T4 3.0, T4 6.0, T5 4.0 (each question stands alone). Selangor Matematik K1 looks more clustered (15 items seen).

## 8. Limitations and confidence

| Limit | Effect |
|---|---|
| 2025 only; 1-2 bodies per subject; 3 bodies at most (SBP, Selangor, Kedah) | Patterns seen in one body are single-source: Matematik Tambahan K1, Fizik K2, Sejarah K2, Geografi K1, Sains Komputer |
| Trial papers, not LPM | Official format may differ in small details; no JSU document seen |
| Page images (scans), read at about 50% zoom | S, TP and unit labels are judgement; second raters would disagree on borderline S2/S3 |
| Partial reads | Matematik K1 Selangor 15 of 40; Fizik Bahagian C rule (inferred, supported by the Biologi cover); Sains Komputer B4 marks; Sejarah skema Q7-9 and Q5(b) |
| Skema read for Matematik, Matematik Tambahan, Fizik, Sejarah (section 10) | Marks confirmed; the skema carry no TP, cognitive level, KBAT, topic or JSU, so those labels stay analyst judgement; no skema was read for Geografi or Sains Komputer |

| Finding | Confidence |
|---|---|
