## Code review: Majors (must fix)

- The contract header says no mock exists, but the paper builder mock is in src/modules/m28-bijak-spm/{server/admin.ts, domain/admin-types.ts, domain/paper-builder.ts, admin/papers} on student-prototype (commit 1507044). The wire shapes were derived without it. The card itself says the lead aligns the future UI through this PR, so the lead should either accept the contract and adapt the UI, or have the contract aligned to the mock. The header comment is wrong either way.
- Paper status differs from the mock. The mock uses AdminPaperStatus 'draf' | 'terbit' (admin-types.ts:167). The contract uses draft | ready | published | withdrawn. Unpublish in the mock sets the status back to 'draf'; the contract's withdraw sets 'withdrawn'. The mapping to the mock needs deciding.
- The mock's paper model is not the contract's. The mock's AdminPaper keeps sections with questionIds arrays, where the section structure is fixed by the blueprint. PaperPatch is {title?, year?, durationMin?, sections?: [{id, questionIds}]}, and createPaper takes {subject, kertas, title?} and returns the new id. The contract instead has separate PUT /sections and PUT /placements, placement objects (pinnedVersion, marksOverride, numberLabel) and a 15-field header body (code, instructions, reference, blueprint ref). A single PUT /admin/papers/:id cannot carry the mock's sections patch, and creation and list fields differ: the mock summary has budget and ok, and updatedAt is a number where the contract uses a timestamp.
- The publish gate differs. The mock's publishPaper returns {ok:false, errors:string[]} as a normal result, with BM lines built from checkPaper, for example 'Soalan berulang dalam kertas: ...' and 'Kumpulan rangsangan X dipecahkan antara dua bahagian'. The contract answers 422 VALIDATION_ERROR with details.issues and details.shortages. The mock's conformance model is SectionCheck {label, count, need, marks, budget, errors, notes, ok}. The contract's is rule-coded issues plus sectionChecks. These BM strings are not carried over.
- Error BM messages differ from the mock. For a locked paper the mock says 'Kertas Terbit tidak boleh disunting. Tarik balik dahulu.' and the contract says 'Kertas yang telah diterbitkan tidak boleh diubah. Tarik balik kertas dahulu.'. For deleting a published paper the mock says 'Tarik balik kertas Terbit sebelum memadam' and the contract says 'Hanya kertas yang masih draf boleh dipadam.'. The mock's 'Tajuk kertas diperlukan', 'Soalan tidak sah untuk kertas ini', 'Bahagian tidak sah' and 'Tiada blueprint untuk subjek dan kertas ini' have no counterpart in the contract. Only 'Kertas tidak ditemui' matches.
- Mock behaviours not covered. deletePaper is idempotent in the mock (an unknown id returns OK), while the contract returns 404. previewQuestions (student-view preview of questions, stimuli and option banks) and the builder's picker candidates and groups (BuilderQuestion, BuilderGroup, getPaperEditor.questions and groups) have no contract endpoint and no explicit note saying they are served by M04 or BE-09. The mock's blueprint read (BlueprintView) is slimmer than the contract's blueprint admin shapes, and blueprint admin write has no mock at all.
