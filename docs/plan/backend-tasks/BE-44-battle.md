## Goal

Everything the prototype serves under `/api/battle/*`, the tournament banner/list/detail/create routes and the battle standings: the battle lobby, quick match and school battle queues, invitations, forfeit, the 10-question arena with server-side scoring, battle history, student and school standings (computed from finished battles), the school battle page, and tournaments. Opponents in queues are **deterministic bots, exactly as the mock codes them** (LEAD rule, TRACKER D53). Real-time multiplayer is not built (Open question 2).

**Why this split.** M16 has 52 mock routes (`handlers/battle.ts` 16 incl. the factory `start(mode)` that registers `quick-match` and `school-battle` from one code line, `standings.ts` 4, `tournaments.ts` 5, `games.ts` 20, `leagues.ts` 5, `admin-games.ts` 2). BE-44 takes the routes whose data is battle records (25: battle 16 + standings 4 + tournaments 5). BE-45 takes the games hub, rooms, solo games, Roda Nasib, Jutawan, Papan Bijak quick match, leagues, Sekolah Bersaing and admin game settings (27). The two cards use separate Nest module folders, Prisma files, contract folders, seeds and test folders, and every table has one owner card. Tournaments sit here (not in BE-45) because they are school-vs-school battle events, share the banner with the battle shell and share no table with the games.

## Acceptance criteria

Replay the cases of the mock tests listed in Inputs. Exact BM messages are those of the mock.

1. **Guards.** No session: 401 `UNAUTHORIZED` "Unauthenticated." on every route. M16-43 and M16-44: 403 `FORBIDDEN` "This action is unauthorized." for every non-admin role. Every other route is reachable by any signed-in role. A mutating call without CSRF gets 403 `CSRF_INVALID` (the global guard; same envelope as everywhere).
2. **Response styles.** Raw routes (M16-02 to M16-14, M16-01 array) return the un-enveloped body of the catalogue; env routes return `{data}`. The raw routes M16-04 to M16-14 answer errors as `{"error": "<BM text>"}` with the status of the catalogue (422 validation, 403 forbidden, 409 conflict, 404 anything else). M16-02 and M16-03 answer 422 and 403 with an **empty body `{}`** (BR-M16-03; the text is not sent). Env routes use the standard `{error: {code, message, fields?}}` body. The contract declares both error shapes per route; every response in tests is parsed by its contract schema. `GET /api/tournament/active` returns a bare array.
3. **Start a queue (M16-02, M16-03).** Body `question_type` (`biasa|pksk|spm`), `open_to_all` (true only when literally `true`). `biasa`: `subject_id` must be a battle subject (Open question 4). `pksk`: active domain and `pksk_target_level` `T1|T4`. `spm`: caller grade at least 10 (Tingkatan 4 or 5), else 403 `{}`; subject with a non-empty SPM pool, else 422 `{}`. The server first checks that 10 eligible keyless questions can be drawn (pool rule `battle-pool.ts poolFilter`: `biasa` = published MCQ of the subject at the owner's level; `spm` = SPM pool MCQ of the subject; `pksk` = domain and level), else 422 `{}`. Success: 200 `{queue_id}` and one `waiting` queue row owned by the caller. `school-battle` stores `mode = school_battle`.
4. **Poll (M16-04).** Only the owner sees the queue; anyone else gets `{status: "not_found"}` (never an HTTP error). A queue older than 120 s, or not polled for more than 10 s, answers `{status: "expired"}`. A queue in `waiting` answers `waiting` for the first two polls and **matches a bot on the third poll** (`wait_polls` counter; constants `MATCH_ON_POLL = 3`, `QUEUE_EXPIRES_MS = 120000`, `QUEUE_ABANDONED_MS = 10000` in `mock/data/constants.ts`), creating an `active` battle with 10 questions and answering `{status: "matched", battle_uuid}`; later polls repeat the same `matched` answer. A `joiner` with outcome `pending` turns the queue into `incoming_challenge` and the poll answers `{status: "incoming_challenge", challenger: {name, grade_label, school}}` (no user id). The "incoming bot after the 2nd poll" of the mock is a test-hook mode, not default behaviour, and is not built.
5. **Bot opponent (deterministic).** The bot is picked from active students (BE-05 service, never a direct table read): same grade as the owner unless `open_to_all` (or the owner has no grade); of another school for `school_battle`; the search widens (ignore grade, then ignore school) when nobody fits; nobody at all gives 404 `{error: "Tiada lawan dijumpai."}`. The choice and the bot's answers come from named PRNG streams (`m16:battle:<queueId>` for the pick, `m16:battle:<battleUuid>` for answers) so a seeded test reproduces them. Open question 3 covers who may be a bot in production.
6. **Accept and reject challenger (M16-05, M16-06).** Accept requires the owner and status `incoming_challenge`, else 404 `{error: "Giliran tidak dijumpai."}`; it creates the battle (owner = A, challenger = B), queue `matched`, answer `{matched: true, battle_uuid}`. Two concurrent accepts create exactly one battle (row lock, test). Reject: owner only; queue returns to `waiting` with the challenger cleared and the wait counter reset; answers `{success: true}` also when the queue is not in `incoming_challenge` (no-op); unknown or foreign queue 404 "Giliran tidak dijumpai.".
7. **Join queue and poll challenger (M16-12, M16-13).** Join: not the owner, queue `waiting` or `incoming_challenge`, no pending join, else 404 `{error: "Ralat menghantar permohonan."}`; success `{success: true}`. Poll challenger: only the joiner; answers `waiting|matched|rejected|expired|not_found` (+ `battle_uuid` when matched), never an HTTP error; when the owner accepted and no battle exists yet it creates it (owner = A, joiner = B) exactly once.
8. **Search and invitations (M16-07 to M16-11).** Search: fewer than 2 trimmed characters returns `{users: []}`; otherwise students whose name contains the text (case-insensitive), excluding the caller, sorted by name then id, at most 10, each `{id, name, school, avatar|null}` (the mock exposes full names, Open question 7). Invite: receiver must be an existing student other than the sender, else 422 `{error: "Penerima tidak sah."}`; same pool pre-check ("Soalan tidak mencukupi."); same type/subject/domain/level messages ("Jenis soalan tidak sah.", "Subjek tidak sah.", "Domain PKSK tidak sah.", "Tahap PKSK tidak sah."); an SPM invite from a caller below Tingkatan 4 is 403 `{error: "Khas untuk pelajar Tingkatan 4 & 5 sahaja."}` (here the text **is** sent); success `{success: true}`. List: the caller's `pending` invitations younger than 24 h, newest first, `{invitations: [{uuid, sender_name, sender_school}]}`. Accept: receiver only, pending, within 24 h, else 404 `{error: "Cabaran tidak dijumpai."}`; creates an `active` battle (sender = A, receiver = B, mode `challenge`) and answers `{success: true, battle_uuid}`. Decline: receiver only, pending; `{success: true}`; else the same 404.
9. **Forfeit (M16-14).** A player of the battle only: unknown or foreign uuid 404 `{error: "Battle tidak dijumpai."}`; battle not `active` 409 `{error: "Battle sudah tamat."}`; success sets `forfeited`, the opponent wins, `finished_at` set, answers `{success: true}`.
10. **Lobby (M16-15).** `{data: {grade, gradeLabel, spmUnlocked, subjects[], pkskDomains[], spmSubjects? }}` as the catalogue: grade label "Darjah n" for 1 to 6, "Tingkatan n" for 7 to 11, empty string otherwise; `spmUnlocked` when grade is at least 10; `spmSubjects` only when unlocked and only subjects with a non-empty SPM pool; `core` is true for the core subjects (Open question 4).
11. **Arena (M16-16, M16-17).** View: players only, others 404 `NOT_FOUND` "Battle tidak dijumpai."; `question` has `id`, `text` and four options `{id 0..3, label A..D, text}` and **never an answer key**; `secondsLeft` is 15 while active, 0 otherwise; `result` present when not active. Answer: body `{questionId, optionId 0..3|null, seconds 0..15}`; `questionId` must equal the caller's next question; errors 422 `VALIDATION_ERROR` "Soalan tidak sah." (`fields.questionId`), "Jawapan tidak sah." (`fields.optionId`), "Masa tidak sah." (`fields.seconds`); 404 "Battle tidak dijumpai." / "Soalan tidak dijumpai."; 409 `CONFLICT` "Battle sudah tamat.". Points for a correct answer: `1000 - round(500 * s / 15)` with `s = floor(seconds)` clamped to 0..15; wrong or timeout 0. The bot's answer for that question is read from its pre-drawn list (`seconds = min(15, floor(2 + 10u))`, correct when `u2 < p`, `p` = platform answer accuracy read once per process start from the practice owner's service, 0 when there are no answers). After question 10: `status = finished`; winner = higher total; equal and non-zero = draw (`null`); **0-0 = player B** (DV-M16-35); `finished_at` set. The response carries `correct`, `correctOptionId`, `points`, `you.score`, `opponent {answered: true, correct, score}` and then `next` (keyless) or `result {winner, scoreA, scoreB}`. Replaying an answered position is not accepted twice (unique `(battle_id, user_id, position)`; test with two concurrent identical answers: one success).
12. **M14 report.** At the end of a battle (finished or forfeited) the caller is reported to M14 `onGameResult` with game `battle`, event `win`, `finish`, `forfeit` or `forfeit_win`, coins 0, xp 0, `correct`, `total`. With coins 0 and xp 0 this changes no wallet or ledger row, so if the M14 entry point is not available yet (Open question 1) the call is a no-op behind the port and no test depends on it.
13. **Standings (M16-18 to M16-21).** Only `finished` and `forfeited` battles count, windows in `Asia/Kuala_Lumpur` (week from Monday, calendar month; injected `Clock`). Students: `period` `weekly` (default) or `monthly`, any other value = weekly; row `{rank, userId, name, school, menang, seri, kalah, ketepatan, streak, skor}` with `skor` = sum of `round(score / 100)` per side, `ketepatan` = round(correct / answered * 100), `streak` = consecutive wins from the newest battle, order by skor desc, menang desc, user id; not paginated; internal school id not in the body. Schools: `period` `weekly|monthly|all_time` (absent = weekly; any other value = all time with `activePeriod: null`), optional `state` exact filter on rows; `rows[]` and `podium[]` (top 3 of the **national** list, not filtered by state); rank by skor desc, then school id; "#" stays the national position under a state filter. School page: `{school, allTime, week, month, fighters[] (top 5 weekly students), recent[] (10 latest finished battles)}`, unknown school 404 `NOT_FOUND` "Sekolah tidak dijumpai.". History: the caller's finished/forfeited battles newest first, unpaged, rows `{uuid, playerA {id, name, homeHere}, scoreA, scoreB, playerB {id, name}, winner, createdAt}`. The school of a player in standings is the school stored on the participant row for school standings, and the player's **current** school for student rows (as the mock does).
14. **Tournaments (M16-01, M16-42 to M16-45).** Active list: tournaments whose stored status is `registration` or `ongoing`; a `registration` tournament whose `registration_closes_at` has passed is returned as `ongoing` (derived on read, no job); order `registration_opens_at` (null last) then `created_at`; drafts and completed excluded; `live_match` is `null`. M16-01 uses snake_case `live_match: {school_a, school_b}`, M16-45 camelCase `liveMatch` (as the catalogue). Detail: drafts visible only to `admin` (others 404 `NOT_FOUND` "Tournament tidak dijumpai."). ## Open questions

Do not guess these; write your working assumption in the PR and ask the lead (issue labelled `question`) if it blocks you.

1. **Who exports the M14 entry points `onGameResult` and `canEarnSyiling`?** The M14 contract (ADR-0014, build plan) names them, but neither BE-11 nor BE-23 lists them. BE-44 only needs `onGameResult` with coins 0 and xp 0 (no observable effect), so working assumption: code against a small port (`GameResultPort`) with a no-op binding until the owner exists. BE-45 depends on the same entry point with real coins; the lead assigns the owner (default proposal: BE-23).
2. **Real-time multiplayer is not invented.** The mock polls (3 s) and simulates every other player with bots; there is no websocket, no matchmaking between two real people and no push. Production behaviour (real matchmaking, websocket, bot fallback) is OQ-API-B4-11. Working assumption: ship exactly the mock's polling contract and deterministic bots; a real second player enters a battle only through invitation accept, join-queue accept or a challenger accept, as the mock already allows.
3. **Who is a bot?** The mock's bot is an existing fictitious student chosen from the users dataset and shown with that student's name. With real pupils that would present a real child as an opponent who never played. Working assumption: replicate the pick through the BE-05 service, store `is_bot = true` on the participant, and do not expose the flag in responses (the client does not show it). The lead must decide before production whether bots use dedicated accounts or a display name.
4. **Subject ids.** The mock restricts battle subjects to M03 ids 1..31 and marks ids up to 7 as `core`; room and tournament code use the same literals. v2 ids are opaque strings. Working assumption: use the active subjects BE-08 serves with the same `core` flag and map by subject code; the exact set (the "31") comes from the BE-08 seed.
5. **Stored school figures.** The mock shows stored Ketepatan/Skor for 8 observed schools (DC-M16-03) plus runtime increments. The real backend computes from battle rows, so those 8 rows will differ from the mock until enough battles exist. Working assumption: no table for the stored figures; list the deviation.
6. **PKSK battles.** `question_type = pksk` needs PKSK domains and levels, but M07 PKSK is backlog (tags on the one bank, plan 14.7 "Out of B1"). Working assumption: keep the columns (no FK), draw through the BE-09 tag filter if BE-09 exposes it, otherwise `pkskDomains` in the lobby is `[]` and `pksk` start answers 422 `{}`. The lead confirms.
7. **Privacy of minors (confirmed by the user, D54(d), 2026-10-19: closed).** `search-opponent`, standings, history and room lists expose full names and schools to any signed-in student (OQ-API-B4-4; SRS SEC-M16-03, -05, -07 recommend restricting search, rate limits, first name + initial and opt-out). LEAD decision 2026-10-19, confirmed by the user (D54(d)): replicate the live/mock behaviour, so classmates see each other's display names on leaderboards as the old system does; seeds and fixtures use fictitious names only; every query parameter is validated (SEC-M16-09). A hardening card before production and a `security-engineer` review remain recommended.
8. **Tournament life cycle.** No route or job moves a tournament beyond `registration`, registers participants, schedules matches, scores or sets `live_match` (OQ-API-B4-13). Working assumption: derive `ongoing` from the close date on read as the mock does and build nothing else.
9. **Data model docs.** M16 tables sit in data model section 3.13 (backlog). The lead moves them into a new section (proposed 3.14 "Battle and games"), adds them to the ownership table 2.2 and the Prisma file list, and adds the lane row to sessions.md section 5. This card does not edit those files.
10. **`.cloud` mode `1v1`.** The migration document lists `1v1` among the `.cloud` modes. Working assumption: not mapped; the ETL (BE-29) rejects it to `etl_rejects` until the lead gives a mapping. Whether completed `.cloud` battles are migrated at all is the lead's decision (data migration 4.10).
11. **Matrix row 18 "Kedudukan".** OQ-API-B4-12: M08 `/ranking` or M16 standings. The routes here are built either way; the frontend decides which menu item calls them.
12. **Consumers.** BE-20 (M11) and BE-22 (M17) need `BattleReadService`; their cards do not list it yet.
