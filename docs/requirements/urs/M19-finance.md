**Conventions** (as M01–M18)
- Quoted UI copy is the exact live text. English or mixed words that live shows ("Billing Dashboard", "Dunning Log", "Grace Period", "Pending", "Bill Code", "Gateway Bill Code", "Gateway Ref", "Gateway", "Event", "Grace Start", "Grace End", "School", "Individual", "Monthly", "Onetime", "Popular", "Unlimited", "Via PPD", "bulk", the Laravel pager "« Previous", "Next »", "Showing … to … of … results", and the raw enum values "personal", "regular", "monthly", "onetime", "toyyibpay") are live copy and are kept. Where CSS capitalises a text this is stated.
- **OBSERVED** = seen on the live site with the admin account (walk-through, inventory state, snapshot, screenshot). **SOURCE-DERIVED** = read from page markup or script captured read-only while the state itself never rendered (the bulk-delete confirm text). **INFERRED** = the most likely live behaviour, not observed; no new UX is invented. **PROPOSED** / **PROPOSED COPY** = a value, layout or text written for the rebuild where live gives nothing (mainly the simulated payment outcome required by D4 and D14); listed in §9 OQ-M19-11 for the Delivery Lead to report at G1.
- **D-M19-n** = live defect of discovery §8.2; **DV-M19-nn** = fidelity deviation (§7), anything not listed there must match live; **DC-M19-nn** = documented display constant (SRS §6.6); **SEC-M19-nn** = security / privacy item (SRS §11.1); **XR-M19-nn** = request to another module's owner (SRS §6.12); **C1–C10** = discovery cross-module conflicts (§5.3).
- **Personal data (privacy overrides fidelity):** payers are students and parents, many of them minors. Discovery tokenised every payer (`<payer:1>`–`<payer:30>`), e-mail, bill code and gateway reference. The rebuild seeds **fictitious payers** ("Pembayar Demo 01" … "Pembayar Demo 30", `pembayarNN@demo.test`), bill codes in the **observed structure** `JB-{REGULAR\|PERSONAL}-{MON\|ONE}-{userId}-{8 mixed-case alphanumerics}` with the seeded payer id and a **fictitious random part**, gateway bill codes of 8 fictitious lowercase alphanumerics (both from a deterministic PRNG), and a fictitious WhatsApp number (DV-M19-09). No real name, e-mail, phone, bill code or payment reference is written anywhere. School names appear only as observed public anchors (the M02 subscription table).
- "Admin" = `admin@demo.test` (canonical 1). **Clock:** the single project clock **2026-09-25T15:40:00+08:00** (architecture §6.12). Every time-dependent figure is computed at that clock and nothing is dated after it; the 2026-09-27 04:23 readings of discovery are the **observed record**. Every M19 figure has the same value at both instants — the newest payment was created on 13 Sep 2026 and the newest dunning log was sent on 21 Jul 2026 — as the reconciliation shows (SRS §6.9 R10, R13, R17, R18): "Hasil Bulan Ini" is RM90.00 (payment #65, paid 13 Sep 2026 12:35, before the clock), "7 Hari Lepas" is 0, and the next reminders due (payment #65's chain, 6 Oct 2026 08:00; #25's chain, 3 Jan 2027 08:00) fall after the clock.

## 1. Overview

M19 is the money side of PRESTASIPLUS. Administrators see every **payment** (list with global figures, filters, detail, a status override and single / bulk delete), a **billing dashboard** (active school subscriptions, active individual subscriptions, grace period, this month's revenue, active school packages, the 20 newest payments) and the **dunning log** of subscription reminders. Every signed-in user sees the **pricing page** (four packages, school packages by level, comparison table, FAQ) with three "Langgan …" buttons, and an **invoice history**. On live the buttons create a pending payment and send the user to ToyyibPay; under **D4** and the user's G1 decision **D14** the rebuild keeps the pending payment and resolves it by a simulated success / failed outcome with no gateway or external screen, then shows the result on S7 (FN-M19-5.2.2–5.2.4, PROPOSED).

The TRACKER listed 5 M19 routes; the crawl found **7 screens** (2 new routes), 4 write endpoints (never submitted on live) and 1 download.
