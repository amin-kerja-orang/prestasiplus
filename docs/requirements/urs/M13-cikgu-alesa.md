## 1. Overview

M13 is **Cikgu Alesa**, the AI assistant of the teacher portal. The TRACKER checklist has 4 routes; the crawl found **8 screens**, because the RPH and essay lists each open a create page and two error outcomes are reachable:

1. **Chat** (`/cikgu`): one conversation box with a greeting, a text input (`required`, max 4,000), a "Hantar" button, a microphone button (speech-to-text) and a "Voice Mode" switch. A turn is one JSON call to `POST /cikgu/send`; the reply is plain text; a meta line shows tier, turns remaining, tool hops and tokens. The thread lives only in a hidden field — a reload starts a new chat. `?ask=` pre-fills the input.
2. **RPH** (Rancangan Pengajaran Harian): a list (`/cikgu/rph`) and a generator form (`/cikgu/rph/baharu`, new route) that posts to `POST /cikgu/rph` and takes 10–30 s on live.
3. **Essay marking**: a list (`/cikgu/essay`) and a form (`/cikgu/essay/baharu`, new route) that hands the essay to the chat, where the `mark_essay` tool marks it with one of 6 rubrics.
4. **PBD Auto-Tag** (`/cikgu/pbd`): a review queue of AI-suggested Tahap Penguasaan (TP1–TP6) per student and SP, with "Jalankan Auto-Tag" (`POST /cikgu/pbd/rollup`), bulk approval (`POST /cikgu/pbd/bulk-approve`) and a CSV export of approved rows.
5. **Error outcomes**: the global 404 page (unknown `/cikgu/*` paths, record ids) and the framework 405 page (GET on the three POST-only routes).
