## 1. Scope

The M17 prototype implements, against the mock service layer, the eleven screens of URS §1: the admin dashboard S1, the standalone Command Center S2 with its drill-down component S4–S7 (also mounted by M10 on `/guru/command-center/*`), the daily-activity board S3, the executive report S8 and its school focus S9, the question analytics S10 and the statistics report S11; plus the mount of the M11 monitoring component on `/admin/monitoring*`, and (v1.13) the school-scope alias of S2–S7 for the Admin Sekolah under `/admin-sekolah/command-center*` (§3.2; UNVERIFIED on live). Every figure is computed per request from the owners' datasets through the named queries of §6.4 at the shared mock clock, or is one of the documented display constants of §6.3. M17 owns one store: the executive-report cache (DATA-M17-01).

**Out of scope:** URS §8 — M11 monitoring content, M23 `/admin/performance`, M12 Kirkpatrick pages, the owners' stores, the global admin shell, real CSV contents and cache infrastructure, the backend.
